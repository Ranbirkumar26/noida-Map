About this model

Every building, road, metro line, river and field is placed from open map data on one metric grid centred between Noida and Greater Noida. The detailed area is 32 × 48 km; around it the wider region is shown as satellite imagery.

Where building heights come from

Real

  • Building footprints and positions
  • Roads, expressways, rail and metro alignments
  • Terrain, rivers and canals
  • Ground and roof colour, from satellite imagery
  • Tree cover
  • Sun position for Noida on 15 November

Generated

  • Windows, facades, rooftop tanks and stair heads
  • Tree shapes, traffic, street and window lights
  • Heights of buildings with no mapped height

Limits

  • Most buildings here have no surveyed height. Satellite estimates stop near 100 m, so only towers with a published height stand taller.
  • Some new societies have no mapped footprint; their blocks are outlined from the satellite height data and look rougher.
  • Ground imagery is 10 m per pixel, so streets blur up close.
